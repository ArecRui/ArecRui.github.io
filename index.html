I study how people select, interpret, and are shaped by media - from risk communication and news framing to the algorithmic, AI-mediated environments reshaping attention today. Trained in Communication and New Media at City University of Hong Kong, I now work at the intersection of communication research and university governance at Zhongyuan Institute of Science and Technology.
