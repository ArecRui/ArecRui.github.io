I explore how media technologies and artificial intelligence reshape human cognition, communication and social interaction. My research focuses on how people select, interpret and respond to mediated information environments — from media effects and health communication to selective exposure and AI-mediated communication. Trained in Communication and New Media at City University of Hong Kong, I work at the intersection of communication research and higher education practice.
